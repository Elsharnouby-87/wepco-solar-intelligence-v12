ⓘTechnical Basis & MethodologyModel assumptions, data provenance & limitations⌄

Dispatch model & financial reference

The selected design, ESP load, power factor and representative hourly weather determine the modeled generator-off hours. The financial base case uses a separate 5.5-hour/day generator-off assumption. Simulation results are screening estimates, not guaranteed operating hours.

Electrical paths & PV power display

PV DC animation is shown using AC-equivalent dispatched power from the hourly model, not measured DC terminal power. Cable trays, panel layouts and power-flow graphics are conceptual; they are not surveyed as-built plant geometry.

Weather, diesel and equipment assumptions

PVGIS TMY historical climate mosaic (2005–2023), 29.8653°N / 28.5249°E; hourly PV reconstructed from supplied CSV, not measured or forecast. 1-hour interval averages. PCS for the 304 kWp feasibility variant is a provisional 150 kW constraint, not an accepted supplier rating; 210 kVA startup transients are not resolved at this hourly scale. *Diesel saving is genset-off hours × (740 L/day ÷ 24); true fuel curve not available. Generator/PV parallel operation disabled in simulation; PCS, transfer and grid-forming need engineering validation. PVGIS documentation ↗

Preliminary feasibility visualization based on provided technical studies and model inputs; engineering design, controls, electrical protection, safety and commercial assumptions remain subject to validation before implementation.
